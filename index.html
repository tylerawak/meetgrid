How to Use MeetGrid

  1. Create an event — Enter an event name, choose specific dates or recurring days of the week, set a time range, select your timezone, and click Create event.
  2. Share the link — Copy the event link and send it to everyone who needs to submit their availability. No account or sign-up is required for participants.
  3. Fill in availability — Each participant opens the link, optionally enters their name, and clicks time slots to mark them as Available, Tentative, or Unavailable.
  4. Find the best time — The Group availability grid updates in real time. Darker green means more people are free at that slot. Hover over any cell to see exactly who is available.

Tips

  • Use "Days of week" mode for recurring meetings like weekly standups — no specific date needed.
  • Participants can come back and edit their availability at any time using the same link.
  • Times are shown in each participant's local timezone automatically.
  • Events and responses are automatically deleted 30 days after creation.
